## About Convotify

Convotify® is an AI-powered customer communication and sales automation
platform that helps businesses manage conversations, capture and qualify
leads, schedule appointments, and turn more inquiries into customers.

By bringing conversations from multiple channels into one intelligent
workspace, Convotify helps teams respond faster, maintain context, and
focus on the opportunities that matter.

## Core Capabilities

-   **24/7 AI Assistant** --- Automate customer conversations and
    respond to incoming inquiries.
-   **Multichannel Communication** --- Connect WhatsApp, Instagram,
    Facebook, and website chat.
-   **Smart Lead Qualification** --- Organize prospects into Hot, Warm,
    and Cold lead categories.
-   **Appointment Automation** --- Schedule appointments with calendar
    integrations.
-   **CRM & Sales Pipeline** --- Keep customer information and sales
    opportunities organized.
-   **Automated Follow-ups** --- Re-engage prospects and follow up on
    missed appointments.
-   **Human Handover** --- Transfer conversations to your team when
    personal assistance is needed.
-   **Knowledge-Grounded Responses** --- Help keep AI replies aligned
    with your business information.
-   **Multilingual Communication** --- Support customer conversations
    across 95+ languages.
